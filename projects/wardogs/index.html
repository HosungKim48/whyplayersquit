Loud vs decisive

From the latest report. One dot per complaint. Further right means more common as the main reason. Higher means reviewers with that main complaint stopped playing more often than others with similar playtime. Dot size is the number of reviewers whose quit status could be judged.

Stop rate = stopped ÷ (stopped + kept playing), among reviews at least 14 days old (written Sep 10 – Nov 5). Lift = a reason's stop rate minus the rate expected from its reviewers' playtime groups. Faded dots: fewer than 100 reviewers. “Unclear / other” is not shown. Hover or tap a dot for counts. How this is measured.

The complaints that don't make players leave — and the ones that do

Report of Nov 19, 2026 · data through Nov 19, 2026

Cheaters and balance are the loudest complaints but not the ones that come before quitting. Among 10–50h players whose main complaint was the 175 ms ping cap, 70% stopped playing, against 35% for their playtime group.
