About whyplayersquit

whyplayersquit is an independent research project. We study public Steam reviews to answer a question every live game faces: players write thousands of complaints, but which ones actually come before they leave?

Steam reviews are unusual data. Alongside the opinion, each review records how long the person had played and how long they kept playing afterward. That lets us separate complaints players tolerate from complaints that come right before they go. Our method: label every review in every language, check the labels by hand, compare players with similar playtime, and say plainly what the data can't show.

Every report is a free public case file. We use only public reviews; we never publish reviewer IDs or profiles, and quotes are short paraphrases.
